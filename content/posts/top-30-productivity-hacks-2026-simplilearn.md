## Time Blocking for Better Focus

Time blocking is another powerful technique from the **Top 30 Productivity Hacks & Tips That Actually Work in 2026**. It involves scheduling specific blocks of time for different tasks. This helps you stay focused and avoid distractions. For example, you might block off 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. for deep work and 1 p.m. to 2 p.m. for meetings.

This method works best when combined with the Pomodoro Technique. It’s a simple but effective way to stay on track and avoid burnout. By using these time management tools, you can make the most of your day.
