## Actionable Tips to Maximize Your Review Strategy

1. **Ask at the right moment** – Don’t ask right after checkout. Wait 2–3 days. People are more likely to respond when they’ve actually used the product.

2. **Keep it simple** – A one-click review link works better than a 10-question survey. Less friction = more responses.

3. **Respond to every review** – Even the 1-star ones. Show you care. It builds trust faster than any ad.

4. **Use review snippets on your site** – Highlight 3-star or 5-star quotes in your product pages. Social proof sells.

5. **Train your team** – Make sure everyone knows how to respond professionally and warmly. A bot can’t replace empathy.

6. **Track your progress** – Use your software’s dashboard to monitor trends. If you see a spike in complaints about packaging, fix it fast.

7. **Reward loyalty** – Offer a discount for leaving a review. Just don’t make it a requirement. People hate being bribed.
